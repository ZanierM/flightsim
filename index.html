Simulation scope

Educational entertainment, not flight training. Authored cockpit and representative airports; simplified aerodynamics, avionics, procedures and weather. The route is a fictional training routing, not a navigation chart. Engine audio is synthesized. Sky imagery: Poly Haven / Greg Zaal & Jarod Guest (CC0). Captain speech depends on your browser’s installed voices.
